About Areca Palm

The Areca Palm, scientifically known as Dypsis lutescens, brings tropical elegance to any indoor space. With its feathery, graceful fronds and golden stems, it transforms rooms into a peaceful retreat. It's not just beautiful—it's one of the most effective air-purifying plants available.

NASA research confirms that Areca Palms remove more air pollutants than almost any other houseplant. In Bengali homes and offices, it's a statement piece that combines functionality with aesthetic beauty.

Care Guide

💧 Watering

Keep soil consistently moist during growing season, but never waterlogged. Reduce watering in winter. The plant prefers humidity, so mist regularly. Use room-temperature water.

☀️ Sunlight

Prefers bright, indirect light. Place near a window with filtered sunlight. Can tolerate medium light but grows slower. Avoid direct afternoon sun, which may scorch the fronds.

🌡️ Environment

Loves humidity and warmth. Ideal temperature range is 65-75°F (18-24°C). Keep away from cold drafts and sudden temperature changes. Brown leaf tips indicate low humidity.

✨ Air Purification

Removes xylene, toluene, and ammonia from the air. A mature plant can purify up to 100 cubic feet of air daily. Perfect for offices and living spaces where air quality matters.

Why We Love Areca Palm

The Areca Palm represents growth, vitality, and tropical grace. Its tall, elegant fronds create movement and life in any space. In many cultures, palms symbolize victory and prosperity, making the Areca Palm not just a decoration but a symbol of aspiration.

Health Benefit: Beyond air purification, studies show that having plants like the Areca Palm in your living space improves mental well-being and reduces stress. The tropical aesthetic alone makes you feel closer to nature.

Perfect For: Bright corners, living rooms, offices with windows, and anyone who wants to create a tropical oasis at home. Also pet-friendly, making it safe for homes with cats and dogs.
