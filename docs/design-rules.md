# Lagao.shop Design Rules

## Visual Identity

- Rural Bengal warmth
- Premium natural realism
- Cinematic but grounded
- Calm and breathable layouts

## Colors

- Earthy greens
- Terracotta
- Cream tones
- Matte black accents

## UI Style

- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Clean spacing
- Large readable typography

## Product Images

- Centered composition
- Consistent lighting
- No harsh shadows
- Natural imperfections allowed

## Layout Philosophy

- Avoid clutter
- Preserve breathing space
- Mobile-first structure
- Consistent section rhythm

## Brand Feeling

The website should feel like:
“A real premium nursery with soul.”
